My 24/7 AI Video Editor

Claude edits your videos for you: it cuts the dead air and mistakes, adds captions and graphics, and gives you a draft to approve. It runs on Herk Edit Kit, Nate Herk's free video editor. Do the Setup Kit first.

A. Setup Kit: paste this in Claude
Set me up with the 24/7 AI Video Editor from https://github.com/ecommercefastlaunch-debug/work-without-working-kit. Read kits/03-video-editor/SETUP.md and install it for me. I'll allow what's needed.

Installs the editor in Documents/herk-edit-kit, renders a demo video, and helps you add your ElevenLabs key (optional; you paste it yourself). About 10 to 20 minutes.

B. Skills
  • Edit my video: short-form-edit (Reels, Shorts, short ads), edit-video (long videos)
  • Cutting: cut-silences (dead air), cut-mistakes (stutters and retakes, shows each cut first)
  • Visuals: video-storytelling, hyperframes-video-beats, style-library
  • Make from scratch: make-a-video, website-to-hyperframes, motion-showreel

Start a new Code session and choose the herk-edit-kit folder in Documents. The video skills only work there. Then:

Use short-form-edit to turn my clip at [where my clip is] into a 30 to 60 second Reel, with a strong hook, captions and B-roll. Show me the cuts before you render.
Use website-to-hyperframes to make a 20-second promo video from [my website link].
