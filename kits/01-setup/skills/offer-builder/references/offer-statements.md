# Offer Statement Playbook

The formula: **RESULT + FRUSTRATION + RISK KILLED + SPEED & EASE**

A strong offer statement answers the four silent questions every buyer has:

| Part | Buyer's silent question | What the statement must do |
|---|---|---|
| Result | "What do I get out of this?" | Name the transformation or outcome in their words |
| Frustration | "Do you understand my problem?" | Call out the pain or frustration they feel now |
| Risk killed | "What if it doesn't work, or I get scammed?" | Remove the fear with a guarantee, proof, or trial |
| Speed & ease | "How long, and how hard?" | Show it's fast to see results and easy to get or start |

## 1. The four parts in depth

### Result (sell the outcome, not the features)
- Convert features into results: "organic ingredients" → "clear skin you don't have to cover with makeup."
- Go one level deeper: what does the result let them do, feel, or become? (status, confidence, freedom, peace of mind, more money, more time)
- Be specific and measurable where honest: "in 30 days", "2x more inquiries", "save 10 hours a week".

### Frustration (hit the problem)
- Use the exact words from the Market Emotions Map.
- Name what they've already tried that failed: "without another expensive cream that does nothing", "even if you've hired 3 agencies before".
- The "without / even if / no more" constructions are powerful here.

### Risk killed
- Address the #1 fear directly (money wasted, it won't work for me, scam, hassle, embarrassment).
- Pick the strongest promise the business can honestly keep (see §3).

### Speed & ease
- Time to first result ("feel the difference on day 1", "your first draft in 48 hours").
- Ease of buying ("order via Messenger, COD available"), of starting ("no experience needed", "we set it up for you"), and of using ("just 5 minutes a day").

## 2. Templates

Adapt these. Don't fill them in mechanically.

1. **Get [result] in [timeframe], without [frustration], or [risk reversal].**
   "Get glowing, acne-free skin in 30 days without harsh products that burn your face, or your money back."
2. **For [avatar] tired of [frustration]: [result], [speed/ease], [risk killed].**
3. **No more [frustration]. [Result] in [time], [ease]. [Guarantee].**
4. **[Result] even if [objection/past failure]. [Ease detail]. [Guarantee].**
5. **We [do the hard part] so you can [result] in [time]. If [result not met], [risk reversal].** (great for done-for-you services)
6. **Question hook:** "Pagod ka na ba sa [frustration]? [Result] in [time]: [ease], [guarantee]."

Short formats:
- **Hook (≤12 words)**: lead with the result or the frustration.
- **Tagline (≤8 words)**: the positioning in a phrase.
- **15-second spoken pitch**: "You know how [frustration]? We help [avatar] get [result] in [time], [ease]. And if [fear], [risk reversal]."

## 3. Ways to kill risk

Choose what the owner can deliver honestly and sustainably.

**General options**
- Money-back guarantee (full refund within X days)
- Satisfaction or replacement guarantee ("not happy? free replacement")
- Conditional guarantee ("follow the steps; if no result in X days, we refund / keep working free")
- Free trial, free sample, tasting, free first session, discovery call, demo
- Pay after results, milestone payments, pay per result, revenue share (B2B)
- Proof: testimonials, before/afters, case studies, ratings, number of customers, certifications, years in business, media features
- Transparency: clear pricing, no hidden fees, no lock-in contract, cancel anytime
- Process guarantee: on-time delivery or it's free, response within X hours, unlimited revisions
- Safe payment: COD, escrow via marketplace, installment

**Regulated or sensitive industries** (medical, dental, aesthetic, supplements, financial, investment, insurance, legal, real estate, education claims, plus B2B services close to legal or tax advice, such as labor/HR compliance, tax, and accounting): don't guarantee health outcomes, returns, or case results. Kill risk with:
- Licensed professionals, certifications, FDA/regulatory registration (only if true)
- Free consultation or assessment before any commitment
- Transparent pricing and clear process
- Satisfaction-with-service promises (e.g., free touch-up, re-consult), not outcome promises
- Real, compliant testimonials and track record
- "No pressure, no hard sell" assurances

Remind the owner to check their country's advertising rules for the industry before publishing. In the Philippines, for example: the Ad Standards Council, FDA rules for health/beauty products and supplements, and the PRC and professional-society codes for licensed professionals such as doctors, dentists, and lawyers.

## 4. Ways to show speed & ease

- First result or "quick win" timing (day 1, first week, 48 hours)
- Delivery speed (same-day, next-day, express)
- Setup speed (onboarded in 24 hours, live in 7 days)
- Done-for-you / done-with-you elements
- Simple steps ("3 simple steps", "just send a photo")
- Convenience (home service, online, 24/7, near you, order via chat)
- Low effort ("5 minutes a day", "no gym needed", "no tech skills needed")
- Flexible payment (installments, GCash/Maya, COD)

## 5. Examples across industries

**Home bakery (product, local)**
"Fresh ube cheese pandesal, delivered warm to your QC home or office before 8AM: no lines, no stale bread. Not the best you've tried? Your next box is free."
Result: fresh, delicious breakfast · Frustration: lines, stale bread · Risk: next box free · Speed/ease: delivered before 8AM

**Skincare (product, online)**
"Clearer, calmer skin in 21 days, without the stinging and breakouts from products that don't suit sensitive skin. One 3-step routine, 3 minutes a day. Love it or get your money back within 30 days."

**Bookkeeping (B2B service)**
"Tax-ready books every month without you touching a single receipt. Just snap a photo and we handle the rest. First month free if your books aren't up to date in 14 days."

**Fitness coaching (service)**
"Lose your first 5 kg in 8 weeks, even if every diet you've tried has failed, with 30-minute home workouts and Filipino meals you already love. Follow the plan and don't lose weight? We coach you free until you do."

**Dental clinic (regulated)**
"A brighter, more confident smile in one visit, with no long waits and no surprise fees. Free consultation, transparent pricing upfront, and licensed dentists you can trust."

**Marketing agency (B2B)**
"More qualified leads in 30 days, without wasting another peso on ads that don't convert. We build and run everything; you just take the calls. No 12-month lock-in: stay only if it works."

**Online course / coaching**
"Land your first freelance client in 45 days, even with zero experience, with step-by-step daily tasks of 1 hour a day. Do the work and don't land a client? We coach you until you do."

**Real estate (big-ticket, regulated)**
"Own a home near your Makati office this year without overpaying or getting lost in the paperwork. We handle the documents and bank financing for you. Free property shortlist and loan pre-check, no commitment."

## 6. Self-check before delivering

For each statement:
- [ ] Is the **result** clear and desirable within 5 seconds?
- [ ] Does it name a **frustration** the target actually feels (in their words)?
- [ ] Is a real **risk/fear removed**?
- [ ] Is it clearly **fast and easy**?
- [ ] Is it **specific to this business** (not copy-paste generic)?
- [ ] Is every claim **true and deliverable**, and compliant for this industry?
