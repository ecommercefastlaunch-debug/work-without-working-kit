# The 10 Ad Angles

Each angle names the Schwartz principles it uses, a structure, hook formulas, and pitfalls. Hook formulas are starting points. Adapt them to the business's voice and language, and never reuse the same structure twice in one batch.

## 1. Hidden Truth / Educational Hook
**Awareness:** Unaware · **Technique:** Camouflage + Gradualization
**Purpose:** Reach people who ignore ads by giving real value first. It looks like a tip, insight, or mini-lesson.
**Structure:** Surprising fact or mistake → short explanation → the deeper truth that makes them realize they have the problem → soft bridge to the product → soft CTA (follow, DM, learn more).
**Hook formulas:**
- "Most [people like you] don't know this about [topic]…"
- "Stop doing [common habit] if you want [result]."
- "3 signs your [thing] is [problem] (number 2 surprised me)."
**Pitfall:** Don't mention the product in the first half. The value must be real.

## 2. Story Belief Chain
**Awareness:** Unaware → Problem-aware · **Technique:** Gradualization + Identification
**Purpose:** Walk the viewer through a relatable story, step by step, to a conclusion they'd otherwise resist.
**Structure:** Relatable moment → small realization → bigger realization → discovery of the solution or mechanism → result → invitation.
**Hook formulas:**
- "Last year, I [relatable failure moment]…"
- "My customer [name/role] told me something I'll never forget…"
- "Storytime: how a [ordinary thing] changed how I [do X]."
**Pitfall:** Keep every step believable. No sudden leaps. If it's a customer story, it must be real or clearly labeled as a scenario.

## 3. Problem Agitation
**Awareness:** Problem-aware · **Technique:** Intensification (of the pain)
**Purpose:** Make them feel the problem vividly, then offer relief.
**Structure:** Name the pain in their words → agitate it (consequences, the moments it hurts, cost of doing nothing) → turn: "It doesn't have to be this way" → solution + offer → CTA.
**Hook formulas:**
- "If [specific painful moment] happens to you every [time], watch this."
- "Pagod ka na ba sa [frustration]?"
- "[Pain] isn't your fault. Here's what's really causing it."
**Pitfall:** Agitate with empathy, not shame. Always deliver the relief.

## 4. Identification ("This is for you if…")
**Awareness:** Problem-aware · **Technique:** Identification (character + achievement)
**Purpose:** Make the right person feel "this is exactly me." This is the main play in stage-5 markets.
**Structure:** Callout of the persona → 3–4 "you…" details from their daily life → the identity or achievement they want → this product as the bridge → CTA.
**Hook formulas:**
- "This is for the [persona] who [specific trait/situation]."
- "If you're a [role] who's tired of [X], this is for you."
- "POV: you're a [persona] and [relatable moment]."
**Pitfall:** Be specific enough that the persona feels seen. Vague callouts ("for everyone who wants success") fail.

## 5. Why Other Solutions Fail
**Awareness:** Solution-aware · **Technique:** Concentration
**Purpose:** Destroy belief in the alternatives (competitors, DIY, cheap substitutes, doing nothing), then position this product as what they lacked.
**Structure:** "You've probably tried…" → why each alternative falls short (fair and factual) → what's actually needed → this product delivers it → CTA.
**Hook formulas:**
- "You've tried [A], [B], and [C]. Here's why none of them worked."
- "The problem isn't you, it's [category of solution]."
- "Why cheap [product] always ends up costing you more."
**Pitfall:** Attack categories, not named competitors (unless legal and factual). Stay respectful.

## 6. Mechanism / How It Works (Education)
**Awareness:** Solution-aware · **Technique:** Mechanization
**Purpose:** Explain *why* this works so the result feels logical and believable. Key in sophistication stage 3–4.
**Structure:** Promise → "Here's how it works" → the named mechanism in 2–4 simple steps (cause → effect) → why that's different → proof → CTA.
**Hook formulas:**
- "Here's exactly how we [result] in [time]."
- "The reason [result] works isn't [common belief]. It's [mechanism]."
- "We do 3 things differently: [1], [2], [3]."
**Pitfall:** Avoid jargon. Use a visual metaphor if possible.

## 7. Objection Crusher
**Awareness:** Product-aware · **Technique:** Redefinition
**Purpose:** Tackle the #1 objection head-on and reframe it.
**Structure:** State the objection out loud (in their words) → validate it → redefine it (reframe the price, time, effort, or doubt) → proof → risk reversal → CTA.
**Hook formulas:**
- "'Mahal naman.' Let's talk about that."
- "'Will this work for me?' Here's the honest answer."
- "The #1 reason people don't [buy/book], and why it's actually a reason to."
**Pitfall:** Pick ONE objection per ad. Make it a different objection in the next batch.

## 8. Proof & Transformation
**Awareness:** Product-aware · **Technique:** Intensification + Verification
**Purpose:** Let results do the selling, with before and after, testimonials, demonstrations, and numbers.
**Structure:** Result-first hook → before state → what they did → after state (specific) → "you can too" → offer → CTA.
**Hook formulas:**
- "[Customer] went from [before] to [after] in [time]."
- "[Number] [customers] later, here's what they all say."
- "Watch what happens when we [demonstration]."
**Pitfall:** Use only real results. If the business has none yet, write placeholders `[insert real result]` and flag them, or use a live demonstration instead.

## 9. Dream Outcome Vision
**Awareness:** Product-aware / any · **Technique:** Intensification (of the desire) + Identification (achievement)
**Purpose:** Paint the "after" life so vividly they want to step into it.
**Structure:** "Imagine…" or a future-pacing scene → sensory details of the dream (what they see, feel, what others say) → the bridge (product) → how fast and easy it is to get there → CTA.
**Hook formulas:**
- "Imagine [dream moment] (without [frustration])."
- "What would you do with [extra time/money/confidence]?"
- "6 months from now, you could be [dream state]."
**Pitfall:** Keep the dream believable and tied to what the product really delivers.

## 10. Direct Response Offer
**Awareness:** Most-aware · **Technique:** Direct claim (stage-appropriate) + the full offer formula
**Purpose:** Close warm audiences (retargeting, followers, past inquiries) with the offer itself.
**Structure:** Offer name + main result → what they get → price or deal → risk killed → speed & ease → real urgency or scarcity → one CTA.
**Hook formulas:**
- "[Offer name]: [result] in [time], [price]."
- "If you've been waiting to try [product], this is your sign."
- "Last [real limit] for [month]: [offer]."
**Pitfall:** Real deadlines and limits only. Make the CTA impossible to misunderstand.

## 11. Example (abbreviated)

Business: handmade genuine-leather bags from Marikina, ₱3,500–₱6,000, sold on IG/FB. Main pain: bags that peel. Main fear: "is it really genuine?" Risk killer: "Real Leather or Refund" + 1-year stitching repair.

1. **Hidden Truth:** "If your 'leather' bag is peeling, it was never leather. Here's how to tell in 10 seconds." → water drop, edge, and smell test → "That's why we show you the leather on video before you pay."
2. **Story Belief Chain:** "I bought 3 bags in 2 years. All peeled…" → realization that cheap means replacing → found Marikina craftsmen → "this one is 3 years old and looks better."
3. **Problem Agitation:** "Yung strap na bumigay sa MRT, habang dala mo laptop mo…" → the cost of replacing bags yearly → relief.
4. **Identification:** "This is for the working girl na laging may dalang laptop, payong, tumbler, at baon…"
5. **Why Others Fail:** "Mall 'leather' bags, Shopee 'genuine' bags, and ₱15k designer bags, and why none of them are what you need."
6. **Mechanism:** "Why our bags don't peel: full-grain hide, hand-stitched edges, burnished, not painted."
7. **Objection Crusher:** "'₱5,000 for a bag?' That's ₱4.50 a day for 3 years."
8. **Proof:** "Carla's bag: day 1 vs year 2." [real customer photos]
9. **Dream Vision:** "Imagine reaching for the same bag every morning for 5 years, and it keeps getting more beautiful."
10. **Direct Offer:** "Marikina Everyday Tote, ₱4,800. Real Leather or Refund + 1-year repair. COD available. Free initials until Dec 15. DM 'TOTE'."
