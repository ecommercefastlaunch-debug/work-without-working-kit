# Kit 03: Org and AI Chief

**Day 1, blocks 7 to 9.** The owner maps who does each job, then hires Grok as their AI
Chief of Staff with everything it needs to know about the business.

## Before this kit

Kits 01 and 02 are done. They're in a Code session with their business folder selected.
Grok Bot is installed and signed in (the facilitator gives those steps in the room).

## The one prompt (owners paste this into Claude)

```
Run Kit 03 from my Documents/work-without-working-kit folder. Read kits/03-org-and-ai-chief/SETUP.md and follow it step by step with me.
```

## What happens

1. Claude maps every recurring job: who does it now, and whether it's for People, a System, or AI
2. The owner corrects the map
3. Claude writes the AI Chief hiring brief: one file with the business, the bottleneck, the org plan, and the Chief's job and rules
4. The owner pastes that brief into Grok Bot
5. Grok explains the business and the bottleneck back; the owner checks it got it right

## They leave with

- [ ] `02 AI Team/org-plan.md`
- [ ] `02 AI Team/ai-chief-brief.md`
- [ ] Grok Bot working as their AI Chief of Staff

## Takes about

60 to 90 minutes, including Grok Bot setup.
