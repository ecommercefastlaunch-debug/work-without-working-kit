# Kit 03: Tools (samples)

Your first tools: ready prompts that use the Sales and Marketing Skills from Kit 02. Nothing to
install. Open a Code session in your **WORK FOLDER**, copy a prompt, fill in the
`[brackets]`, and send. Claude saves what it makes in your project's `Outputs` folder.

Do Kit 01 (Work Without Working Setup Kit) and Kit 02 (Sales and Marketing Skills) first.

| Tool | What you get | Skill it uses |
|---|---|---|
| My Offer | Market research, competitors and a clear offer for what you sell | offer-builder |
| 10 Ads in One Go | 10 ad scripts from 10 angles, ready for Reels or Facebook | breakthrough-ads |
| Product Poster | A 4:5 poster of your product, made with your ChatGPT | image-generation |
| Price Objection Replies | Replies for "mahal" and "pag-iisipan ko muna" you can send on Messenger | sales-objections |
| Follow-Up Messages | 3 follow-ups for people who asked the price and went quiet | sales-followup |
| 30-Day Content Plan | A month of posts with captions for your page | content-strategy |
| 5 Reels Scripts | Short video scripts with hooks, ready to shoot | video-scripts |
| Review Replies | Kind, professional replies to your Google and Facebook reviews | review-response-writer |

## The prompts (copy and paste)

**My Offer**

```
Use offer-builder on my business: [what I sell, who buys it, my price].
```

**10 Ads in One Go**

```
Use breakthrough-ads to write 10 ad scripts for my offer. Write them in Taglish.
```

**Product Poster**

```
Use image-generation to make a 4:5 poster of [my product] with the words "[headline]".
```

**Price Objection Replies**

```
Use sales-objections: my customers say "mahal" and "pag-iisipan ko muna". Give me Messenger replies.
```

**Follow-Up Messages**

```
Use sales-followup: write 3 follow-ups for inquiries who didn't reply after I sent the price.
```

**30-Day Content Plan**

```
Use content-strategy and social-captions: plan 30 days of Facebook posts for my business, with captions.
```

**5 Reels Scripts**

```
Use video-scripts: 5 Reels scripts about [topic], in Taglish, 30 seconds each.
```

**Review Replies**

```
Use review-response-writer on these reviews: [paste the reviews].
```

## Make your own tool

Pick the task that eats your week (your bottleneck), then tell Claude:

```
Make me a tool for [task]. I'll give it [what I give it], and I want back [what I want]. Ask me questions one at a time, then save it as a skill I can use every week.
```

The list of samples lives in [tools.tsv](tools.tsv).
