01

Setup

Do this first · One prompt installs everything · About 15 to 25 minutes

Paste one prompt. Claude installs everything below for you. You only approve when your computer asks.

Set me up with Kit 01 from https://github.com/ecommercefastlaunch-debug/work-without-working-kit. Read kits/01-setup/SETUP.md and install everything for me. I'll allow what's needed.

What's included

Homebrew

Mac only. The Mac's installer for everything below.

Git

Downloads the kits and skills.

FFmpeg

Lets Claude cut and convert your videos.

Node.js

Runs the video editor and other tools.

Google Chrome

The video editor uses it to make videos. Skipped if you have it.

Skills: stop-slop + dumbify

Writing that doesn't sound like AI, and explanations kept simple.

Kit files

The guides for Kits 02 and 03, saved on your laptop.

WORK FOLDER

Your home base, with a file that tells Claude about your business.

What you do

  • Mac: a Terminal window opens. Click OK, type your Mac password (nothing shows while you type), press Return.
  • Windows: say yes to the programs' terms, and click Yes when Windows asks to allow changes.
  • Answer one question: what you sell and who buys it. Copy it from page 3.
  • At the end Claude shows a ✓ for each item. Tick the boxes above.

Your WORK FOLDER

WORK FOLDER/
  CLAUDE.md      your business + the rules
  my-tools.md    your tools (Kit 02)
  <project>/    one per project
    Outputs/    all Claude makes

From now on

  • Start a new Code session and choose your WORK FOLDER.
  • Say what you're working on. Claude makes a folder for it.
  • Everything it makes goes in that project's Outputs.

Skills Directory

Exercise 1: build your own tool · After Kit 01 · About 30 minutes · Run it again for every new tool

Pick a ready tool from 74 (captions, ads, proposals, invoices, product photos and more), or have Claude find or build one. Then make it yours and test it on real work.

Run Kit 02 from my Documents/work-without-working-kit folder. Read kits/02-skills-directory/SETUP.md and follow it step by step with me.

A. From the catalog

Claude suggests 3 to 5 of the 74 tools that fit your bottleneck. You pick one.

B. Something else

Describe the tool you wish you had. Claude searches, checks it's safe, and asks before installing.

C. From scratch

Nothing fits? Claude writes it with you.

1

Your business and project

Claude reads your CLAUDE.md and asks the task that eats most of your time (page 3), and which project this is for.

2

Choose the path

Claude shows the tools that fit, plus "see all", "something else" or "from scratch". You choose.

3

Install or find the tool

Claude asks before installing anything. Tools from other people come from their GitHub, with credit.

4

Make it yours

Three questions, one at a time: a real example, what a great result looks like, and what it must never do. Answers on page 6. Claude names it my-something.

5

Test it on real work

Claude runs your tool on your example and saves the result in your project's Outputs.

6

Improve it once

Say what to change. Claude fixes the tool itself, not just the result, and runs it again.

7

Record it

Your tool is added to WORK FOLDER/my-tools.md. Use it any time: type /my-yourtool in a session.

03

24/7 Video Editor

After Kit 01 · Heavy install, about 30 minutes plus the edit · Built on Nate Herk's Herk Edit Kit

Claude installs the video editor, then edits one of your own clips: dead air and mistakes cut, captions added, a draft for you to approve.

Bring

  • A 1 to 3 minute clip of you talking
  • Your ElevenLabs key, if you have one. You paste it yourself. No key? You still get the demo today.
Run Kit 03 from my Documents/work-without-working-kit folder. Read kits/03-video-editor/SETUP.md and follow it step by step with me.
1

Check the tools

Node.js, Git, FFmpeg and Chrome. Kit 01 installed them, so these should all be found.

2

Install what's missing

Usually nothing. Claude asks before installing.

3

Download the video editor 5 to 15 min

Into Documents/herk-edit-kit, then setup and a test run.

4

Your transcription key

Open herk-edit-kit/.env, paste your ElevenLabs key after ELEVENLABS_API_KEY=, save. Claude never sees or types it.

5

Render the demo

Claude makes the kit's sample video. Play it to see it works.

6

Edit your clip

Open a new Code session with the herk-edit-kit folder and paste the prompt Claude gives you (with your clip filled in). Approve or skip each cut, then watch the draft.

7

Approve and save

Say Approve, or give one fix. The final video goes in your project's Outputs in your WORK FOLDER.

Every new video after today

Open a session in the herk-edit-kit folder, give it your clip, and only review the draft.
