01

Work Without Working Setup Kit

Do this first · One prompt installs everything · About 15 to 25 minutes

Paste one prompt. Claude installs everything below for you. You only approve when your computer asks.

Set me up with Kit 01 from https://github.com/ecommercefastlaunch-debug/work-without-working-kit. Read kits/01-setup/SETUP.md and install everything for me. I'll allow what's needed.

What's included

Homebrew

Mac only. The Mac's installer for everything below.

Git + GitHub app

Downloads files and skills from GitHub.

FFmpeg

Lets Claude cut and convert your videos.

Node.js

Runs tools like video editors and app builders.

GitHub account

No account yet? Claude opens the sign-up page. You sign up and sign in yourself.

WORK FOLDER

Your home base, with a file that tells Claude about your business.

What you do

  • Mac: a Terminal window opens. Click OK, type your Mac password (nothing shows while you type), press Return.
  • Windows: say yes to the programs' terms, and click Yes when Windows asks to allow changes.
  • Answer one question: what you sell and who buys it. Copy it from page 3.
  • GitHub: sign up or sign in yourself, then paste the code Claude shows and click Authorize.
  • At the end Claude shows a ✓ for each item. Tick the boxes above.

Your WORK FOLDER

WORK FOLDER/
  CLAUDE.md      your business + the rules
  <project>/    one per project
    Outputs/    all Claude makes

From now on

  • Start a new Code session and choose your WORK FOLDER.
  • Say what you're working on. Claude makes a folder for it.
  • Everything it makes goes in that project's Outputs.

02

Skills

After the Setup Kit · One prompt adds all 12 · About 2 to 5 minutes

Skills are add-ons that teach Claude a job, like writing your offer or your ads. One prompt adds all of them.

Add my skills with Kit 02 from https://github.com/ecommercefastlaunch-debug/work-without-working-kit. Read kits/02-skills/SETUP.md and install them for me.

Your 12 skills

offer-builder

Market research, competitors and offer statements from what you sell.

breakthrough-ads

10 ad scripts per request, each from a different angle.

copywriting

Offers, landing pages, headlines, product pages.

product-marketing

Who it's for, why you, what to say first.

customer-research

Turns customer messages and reviews into what they really want.

social

Posts, carousels and content calendars.

ad-creative

Ad headlines and copy variations.

content-repurposing

One piece of content into many posts.

hook-killers

Fixes weak opening lines.

storytelling

Gives scripts and posts a story that holds attention.

stop-slop

Keeps the writing from sounding like AI.

dumbify

Keeps explanations and customer messages simple.

How to use them

  • Start a new Code session in your WORK FOLDER. Skills only show up in new sessions.
  • Type / to see them, or just ask: "Use offer-builder on my product."
  • First try: paste what you sell and ask for your offer.
